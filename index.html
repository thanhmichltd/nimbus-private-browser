FAQ
Do I need to create an account?

No. Nimbus has no sign-in or account system at all — install it and start browsing immediately.

Does Ad Block really block every ad?

No, and we don't claim it does. It's a basic, on-device, best-effort filter — it may reduce ads and pop-ups on many sites, but some ads or trackers may still get through on some sites.

Where is my browsing history stored?

On your device, in local app storage. Nimbus doesn't require an account and doesn't sync your history to a server we run.

Can Nimbus be my default browser?

Yes — turn it on from Settings → “Set as default browser.” Search and Home also work fully even before you do.

Is Nimbus free?

Yes, Nimbus is free to download and use.
